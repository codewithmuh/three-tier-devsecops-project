# Accessibility Statement

## Our Commitment

We are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all developers, students, and contributors who interact with the **Three-Tier DevSecOps Project** and its learning materials. We continually improve the user experience and apply relevant accessibility standards.

---

## Measures to Support Accessibility

We take the following measures to ensure accessibility across our documentation, diagrams, and codebases:
* **High Contrast Diagrams**: Architecture diagrams utilize native Mermaid flowcharts with high-contrast text and standard shapes for screen-reader and visual clarity.
* **Semantic Markdown**: All documentation uses structured GitHub Flavored Markdown headers (`#`, `##`, `###`), descriptive lists, and proper table semantics.
* **Descriptive Text for Media**: Badges, schematics, and images include clear, contextual alternative text (`alt` attributes).
* **Color Independence**: Statuses and architectural states are conveyed with text labels and symbols, not color alone.

---

## Standards Alignment

Our documentation aims to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** standards.

---

## Feedback & Barrier Reporting

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this repository. If you encounter any accessibility barriers while using our documentation or code:

* **Email**: [contact@codewithmuh.com](mailto:contact@codewithmuh.com)
* **GitHub Issues**: Open an issue labeled `accessibility`

We strive to respond to accessibility inquiries within 3 business days.
